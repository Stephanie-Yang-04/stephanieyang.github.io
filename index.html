About
Hi, I'm Stephanie
nice to meet you.

I'm a student at the University of Oregon studying business marketing, working at the intersection of strategy and creative expression.

I was born and raised in Taiwan, then moved to Oregon and fell in love with the fresh air of the Pacific Northwest. One of the most meaningful things I brought with me was my passion for visual design. What began as an instinct to draw has grown into a way of thinking that shapes how I approach problems, stories, and systems in my work.

My work lives where analysis meets design. I've led consulting projects, built marketing strategies for nonprofits, and published illustrations through student media. Design adds another layer to how I communicate and bring ideas to life.
